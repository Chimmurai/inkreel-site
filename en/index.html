01

Philosophy

The eye sees only light and shadow.

“Black is not the absence of light. It is a decision.”
— the InkReel principle

Color is a gift of the brain. The eye catches only difference: lighter here, darker there. Take away everything extra and the essence remains: form, direction, rhythm.

White and black do not fight. They hold each other up: without shadow there is no form, without light there is no shadow.

Now unfocus your gaze for a moment. Between black and white countless shades open up: a halftone is an honest trick, the eye assembles it from dots and strokes. The app has “Defocus” for exactly this.

Engravers knew this five centuries ago, and printers know it still. We only taught it to moving images, frame by frame.

InkReel does not paint or decorate. For every speck of the frame it decides — light or shadow? — and stands honestly behind that decision. The same frame, the same ink: no randomness, no algorithm “mood”.

Features

What the ink can do

01

Video, photos, GIFs

Dozens of file types: MP4, GIF, JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC and more. Open with Ctrl+O, drag and drop, or “Open with”.

02

Five quality levels

Sketch, Outline, Halftone, Drawing, Engraving: from a fast threshold to a detailed engraving whose strokes follow form.

03

Polarities and “cinema”

“Two polarities”: the positive and the negative of one frame, divided by an S-curve. “Defocus” melts black and white into shades. “Cinema” mode (the F key or a double click) leaves only the frame.

04

IRNM1

Our own neural network, a separate branch beside the algorithm. Several times faster on a graphics card, and the app picks what to run on by itself.

05

Render all

A custom .ikr format: compute once, then play, zoom and export any frame or moment without recomputing.

06

Export and palettes

MP4 (with sound) and GIF, a frame-rate cap, twenty palettes for every taste. The app remembers your choice.

03

Views

One light, two views.

“Ink” and “Symbols” are two ways to look at one frame. Both use only two colors.

Ink

Dots, strokes and grain, and on the higher levels an engraving that follows the shape of things. Each area of the frame gets its own hand, and clean flat surfaces stay clean.

Symbols

A picture made of letters: each glyph is a real glyph of a monospaced font, chosen by its tone. All five levels work here too.

04

Levels

Five circles and one diamond.

The scale runs from a quick sketch to a detailed engraving. The sixth item is not a higher step but a separate branch.

  1. 1Sketch
  2. 2Outline
  3. 3Halftone
  4. 4Drawing
  5. 5Engraving
  6. IRNM1neural

1–2 · Sketch and Outline

One threshold for the whole frame, or a threshold by neighborhood: crisp lines and text when speed matters. Long videos, weak PCs, drafts.

3–4 · Halftone and Drawing

Tones from blue noise, as in a newspaper, then dots, strokes and grain chosen by what is in the frame. Good for most videos.

5 · Engraving

Strokes follow the shape of things, as in a real engraving. The most detailed level, it protects the small stuff.

IRNM1 · Neural

A neural network that learned from “Engraving”. Not a higher level but a fast branch with its own character.

White is a pause. Black is a word.

FAQ

The short version

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no trial periods.

Do I need a graphics card?

No. The algorithm runs on the CPU. A graphics card speeds up the neural level IRNM1: the app picks what to run on and falls back to the CPU if the card fails.

Why does Windows warn me when I install the .exe?

The installer outside the Store has no paid code-signing certificate, so SmartScreen sees an unknown publisher. The Store version is signed by Microsoft and raises no such warnings.

The first export “thinks” for a long time. Is that normal?

Yes. After installing or updating, the app compiles its compute kernels once: up to half a minute on a weak PC. After that it is fast.

What are .ikr files?

A custom format for a “baked” video: it holds the finished result. Open it in InkReel in player mode and export any moment without recomputing.

Why does the Store version have no Explorer right-click entry?

Store packages cannot add Explorer menu entries the way the installer does. Open files via “Open with”. The installer version has the entry.

Does InkReel send anything to the internet?

No. Everything is computed on your computer; there are no accounts and no telemetry. Details are in the privacy policy.

What about licenses?

The app bundles FFmpeg (GPL), ONNX Runtime and other components. Their licenses are in the app folder and mentioned in the “About” section.
