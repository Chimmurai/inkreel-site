Same frame, two colors.

Every frame in two views: pixel art and a picture made of letters. Sweep a cursor or a finger across a card to change the view; click to open the frame closer.

Animals

Fur, feathers and a gaze in two colors, from an owl to a horse in mist.

Nature

Sky, water, stone and fields: landscapes where black and white keeps only form and light.

City and architecture

Facade lines, staircases, bridges and rainy streets turned into pure ink.

People and silhouettes

Hands, silhouettes and motion without faces: a person as a shape against light.

Plants

Leaves, petals, berries and frost up close: texture that dithering reads well.

Objects with a past

Books, metal type, film and ink: the things black and white grew out of.

Interior and light

A sunbeam in a room, a railing shadow and half-dark: light as the main character.

Food

Bread, fruit, spices and coffee as pixel art and text symbols.

Vehicles

From a rowing boat to a steam locomotive: machines in black and white.

Light and matter

Water, smoke, fire, frost and sand: motion and texture without objects.

Retro tech

Floppies, CRT monitors and cassettes: an era that already thought in two colors.

Space and science

Craters, crystals, a microscope and a shell: the micro world and deep space in two colors.

The source frames were made with an image generator; InkReel turns them into two colors. Pixel art is shown at its real size and enlarged without smoothing; “Symbols” pictures are built from real glyphs of a monospaced font.
